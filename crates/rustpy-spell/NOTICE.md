`src/words.txt` is generated by `tools/build_words.py` from two sources.

The English words come from `words_alpha.txt` of
<https://github.com/dwyl/english-words>, filtered to words of 2 to 20
lowercase ASCII letters (about a hundred entries that are far more often
misspellings than words, such as `recieve`, were dropped; see the generator

## Standard-library vocabulary — typeshed (Apache-2.0)

The remaining entries are lowercase identifier parts harvested from the
typeshed standard-library stubs bundled with `rustpy-semantic`
(`crates/rustpy-semantic/typeshed`, <https://github.com/python/typeshed>),
so that names such as `argv`, `abspath` or `getcwd` are not reported as
typos. typeshed is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0; the full
license text is in `crates/rustpy-semantic/typeshed/LICENSE`.
